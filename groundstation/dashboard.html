…as a distance—

That second number is what you get treating a software UDP round trip as a time of flight. Ten metres of light is 67 ns; this carries milliseconds of Wi-Fi MAC, IP stack and scheduler delay. Averaging does not rescue it — the error is systematic, not zero-mean. Real ranging needs hardware timestamps at the MAC (802.11mc FTM), which is why the flight build ranges between the two S3s on 2.4 GHz and leaves these boards to move data. RTT is a link-health metric, not a ruler.
